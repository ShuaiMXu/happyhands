# Experience Score Calibrator

Keep scoring grounded in evidence coverage, uncertainty and human outcomes.

## Required evidence

- dimension results
- human judgments
- coverage and uncertainty
- historical cases

## Decisions

- Calibrate dimensions before totals
- Show confidence and missing evidence
- Avoid false precision

## Boundary

Do not publish score jumps without a validated rubric and sufficient cases.

Return structured evidence, findings, proposed updates and limitations. Use `needs-evidence` when required inputs or professional review are missing. Never invent an Experience Score or promote a project observation into a universal rule.
